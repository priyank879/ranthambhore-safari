Experience the Wild Majesty of Ranthambhore

Embark on an unforgettable journey through India's premier tiger reserve. Witness the majestic Bengal tiger in its natural habitat, along with diverse wildlife in their pristine ecosystem.

Safari Zones

Zones 1-5 (Core Area)

Highest probability of tiger sightings with diverse terrain including lakes, dense forests and ancient ruins. The most popular zones for safari tours.

High Tiger Density Varied Terrain

Zones 6-10 (Buffer Area)

Larger zones with more open landscapes, ideal for spotting leopards, sloth bears, and various deer species alongside occasional tiger sightings.

Rich Birdlife Open Landscapes

Ranthambhore Fort Zone

Historical zone combining wildlife with 10th-century fort ruins. Unique opportunity to spot animals amidst ancient architecture and panoramic views.

Historical Scenic Views

Safari Booking Information

Ranthambhore National Park offers two safari shifts daily. Advance booking is highly recommended as permits are limited and sell out quickly, especially during peak season (October-June).

  • Morning Safari: 6:30 AM - 10:00 AM
  • Evening Safari: 2:30 PM - 6:00 PM
  • Park closed: Wednesday afternoon & Monsoon (July-September)
  • Photo ID mandatory for all visitors
  • Tiger sightings are natural occurrences, not guaranteed
